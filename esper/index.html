ESPer FIELD PROCEDURE

On Hex graving, Focal and Liminal Phases

HEX PROCEDURE DIRECTIVES

Graving

Hexes are Graved before mission start, after briefing. Roll on each Parameter table. Result is how the ESP manifests in the material world through the Hex. One Hex occupies one inventory slot. Hexes cannot be Graved during a mission as they require extensive study, practice and preparation.


Fixing

At any point, during the operator’s turn, they can choose to Fix a Focal Phase Hex with a Half-Action. By doing that, the Hex enters a cooldown period and can only be used again in the next scene. The operator can, instead, attempt Phase Reversal and use a Full Action to Fix a Liminal Phase Hex. This action can be a Rite Action (multiple turns of uninterrupted rituals, incantations, offering, conjuration, evocation, utterance, litany or markings) to complete the Fixing, or an Observation Action (resolves every round, but requires the operator to concentrate on the rites, taking damage can interrupt it).


Regraving

Fixing a Hex does not remove it from inventory. It remains there until the operator decides to override it with a new one. The operator, before a new mission and after a new briefing, can Grave a new Hex to override an old one.

PHASE REVERSAL — LIMINAL PHASE

The Liminal Phase is not an extension of the Focal Phase. It is its inversion. The same force, accessed through old folkloric ritualistic practices. Unclassified. There is no Hex Grave for Liminal Phase, it is unbound by the scientific logic that governs Focal Phase operation. Much more powerful, and less predictable, requires rites, incantations, offering and sustained effort from the ESPer. If the Focal Phase is optimized for reduced damage to the ESPer, the Liminal Phase is severely taxing.
