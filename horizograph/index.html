KIND-IV OPERATOR
HORIZOGRAPH

ULTRABLACK
ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

§1.1 A KIND-IV Operator (Horizograph) is an individual with no direct ESP capability and no field instrumentation.

§1.2 All interaction with ESP occurs through trace-quantity residue designated AFTERIMAGES.

>> NO EQUIPMENT
>> NO EMISSION
>> RESIDUE ONLY
>> INPUT ACCEPTED
>> SYSTEM INITIALIZED
ARTICLE II — ACQUISITION

§2.1 An Afterimage is acquired only through direct sustained observation of a paracausal event.

§2.2 Acquisition is passive. The Operant does not generate the phenomenon; the Operant witnesses it.

§2.3 Each Afterimage class requires a distinct observation condition. Conditions are non-substitutable.

>> WITNESS CONFIRMED
>> RESIDUE RETAINED
ARTICLE III — RELEASE PROTOCOL

§3.1 A held Afterimage may be released to produce one bounded, temporary effect.

§3.2 Release is terminal. The Afterimage is consumed and cannot be recovered.

§3.3 Effect duration is fixed at acquisition and cannot be extended.

>> SPENT
>> NOT RECOVERABLE
ARTICLE IV — CONSTELLATION FORMATION

§4.1 Two or more specified Afterimages held simultaneously and unreleased form a bound configuration designated a CONSTELLATION.

§4.2 Constellations are:

>> permanent, while intact
>> passive
>> non-stacking with themselves

§4.3 A Constellation confers a fixed modifier or mechanic for as long as every member Afterimage remains held and unreleased.

ARTICLE V — COLLAPSE PRINCIPLE

§5.1 Releasing any Afterimage that is a member of a Constellation immediately and irreversibly collapses that Constellation.

§5.2 Collapse is total. Partial retention does not preserve partial effect.

§5.3 An Afterimage held as part of more than one Constellation, if released, collapses all Constellations of which it is a member.

>> STRUCTURE BROKEN
>> MECHANIC VOID
ARTICLE VI — LIMITATIONS

§6.1 The Operant cannot manufacture, transfer, or duplicate an Afterimage.

§6.2 Retention capacity is finite and classified under Failure Index [REDACTED].

§6.3 The Operant's decision to hold or release remains the sole point of operator agency in the system.

HORIZOGRAPH FIELD PROCEDURE

On sanctioned Afterimage retention and Constellation integrity

OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVES

Acquisition

Afterimages are not equipped, rolled, or selected. Each is acquired in the field by satisfying its observation condition. Conditions are recorded exactly as witnessed. No approximation is permitted.


Retention

An acquired Afterimage is held indefinitely until released. Holding produces no effect on its own. An Afterimage held in isolation grants nothing beyond its potential.


Release

Release is a declared action, resolved immediately. The stated effect applies once and the Afterimage is discarded.

>> 1 Afterimage held → 0 effect
>> 1 Afterimage released → 1 bounded effect, consumed

Constellation Integrity

Constellations exist only while every required Afterimage remains unreleased. There is no partial Constellation state.

>> ALL MEMBERS HELD → CONSTELLATION ACTIVE
>> ANY MEMBER RELEASED → CONSTELLATION COLLAPSED

EXECUTION MODEL

>> Afterimages → small, immediate, consumed
>> Constellations → durable, passive, revocable

Trade Condition

The Operant faces a standing choice at all times:

>> spend now for a small bounded gain
>> hold indefinitely for a standing structural gain

The two cannot be taken together for the same Afterimage.

DESIGN INTENT

Horizographs accumulate rather than generate. They do not act on the field directly; they carry residue of what they witnessed and decide, continuously, whether that residue is worth more spent or worth more kept.

Tactical Consequence

>> Early observation → low inventory, no structure
>> Mid accumulation → partial Constellations, exposed
>> Late retention → full Constellation set, fragile to loss

Field Directive

Protect held inventory. A forced release, self-inflicted or compelled, is structural damage, not merely resource loss.

>> Observe before acting
>> Weigh spend against hold
>> Treat every release as a demolition, not a purchase
AFTERIMAGE TELEMETRY DIRECTORY
Full acquisition condition and release effect, organized by detection category
OPTICAL
AIT-OPT01 — GIMBAL

Call Sign: "Gimbal"

Category: OPTICAL

Acquire: Confirm an anomaly rotating independent of its travel vector under continuous visual contact, full scene duration.

Release: Until scene end, attacks against the Operant gain no bonus from flanking, rear position, or superior positioning.

AIT-OPT02 — GREEN PYRAMID

Call Sign: "Green Pyramid"

Category: OPTICAL

Acquire: Confirm identical impossible geometry from three independent viewpoints.

Release: Until scene end, cover, concealment, and line-of-sight penalties from physical obstacles are ignored.

AIT-OPT03 — JELLYFISH

Call Sign: "Jellyfish"

Category: OPTICAL

Acquire: Confirm an anomaly holding fixed position against active environmental force.

Release: Until next turn, the Operant cannot be knocked prone, pushed, pulled, or forcibly moved.

AIT-OPT04 — HALO

Call Sign: "Halo"

Category: OPTICAL

Acquire: Confirm a luminous ring persisting around an anomaly independent of angle or lighting change.

Release: The next Pale loss this scene is reduced by 2.

THERMAL
AIT-THM01 — TIC TAC

Call Sign: "Tic Tac"

Category: THERMAL

Acquire: Confirm instantaneous acceleration, stop, or altitude change of an anomaly, corroborated by a second witness.

Release: Immediately gain an additional Movement after completing any Procedure.

AIT-THM02 — COLD CORE

Call Sign: "Cold Core"

Category: THERMAL

Acquire: Confirm total thermal absence during active manifestation.

Release: Restore one Pale to its maximum value.

Call Sign: "Blackbody"

Release: The next attack cannot be reduced by armor, resistance, or comparable damage reduction.

AIT-RAD03 — FALSE HORIZON

Release: The next Procedure ignores penalties from invisibility, darkness, concealment, or loss of vision.

Skyhook

Mechanic: FIRE CONTROL SOLUTION

Once each combat, designate one visible creature as LOCKED. Procedures targeting it are Stamped; attacks against it ignore cover; it cannot benefit from concealment.

CST-13 — GRAVEYARD ORBIT

Graveyard Orbit

Requires: Tic Tac + Jellyfish + Negative Parallax + Pressure Scar
Mechanic: ZERO INERTIA

Immunity to forced movement. Whenever another creature would move the Operant, the Operant may instead move the same distance in any direction.

CST-14 — MAJESTIC

Majestic

Requires: Gimbal + Tic Tac + GoFast + Mosul Orb + Ghost Return
Mechanic: SENSOR FUSION

Every Procedure is treated as Stamped. Whenever two d20 are rolled, reroll one of them.

CST-15 — CONSTELLATION 46

Constellation 46

Requires: Green Pyramid + Static Memory + Missing Frame + Observer Drift + Split Echo
Mechanic: PATTERN COMPLETION

Once per scene, declare two visible phenomena manifestations of the same anomaly. The GM must adjudicate them using one shared property for the remainder of the encounter.

CST-16 — CELESTIAL MERIDIAN

Celestial Meridian

Requires: GoFast + Fastwalker + Dead Vector + Negative Parallax + Plasma Wake
Mechanic: ABSOLUTE POSITION

Position cannot be altered by any effect except the Operant's own movement. Movement always ends exactly where chosen.

CST-17 — CONSTELLATION ZERO

Constellation Zero

Requires: Any six unique Afterimage Telemetries
Mechanic: OBSERVER EFFECT

Once per session, after any die is rolled, declare the result unobserved. Erase the roll completely. The action replays from the beginning with entirely new dice. Nothing from the original resolution remains.

CST-18 — OMEGA SKY

Omega Sky

Requires: Gimbal + Tic Tac + GoFast + Black Triangle + Ghost Return + Fastwalker
Mechanic: CONTINUOUS SOLUTION

Permanent LOCK state. Every Procedure is automatically Stamped. Movement never provokes reactions. The first time each scene a Reading would be lost, prevent that damage and instead lose an equal amount of Pale from any Reading of choice.
