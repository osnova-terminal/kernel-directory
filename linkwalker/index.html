LINKWALKER FIELD PROCEDURE

On Knot handling and standard signal deviations

KNOT PROCEDURE DIRECTIVES

Hewing

Knots are Hewed before mission start, after briefing. Roll on the Core Knots table. Result is the knot carried into the assignment. One knot occupies one inventory slot. Knots may be Hewed during the mission. Mid-mission Hewing rolls on the Broken Knots table. Broken Knots are volatile.


Fix

At any point, during the operator's turn, they can choose to Fix a knot. By doing that, they spend the knot and remove it from the inventory permanently.

LINKWALKING PROCEDURE REPORT

Link

Linkwalkers cannot fix or grave Hexes, only hew and fix Knots. Their link to Extrasensory Perception is not self-originating. The signal is always compromised by noise interference. The Kete-Class Object overrides the operator in most module scans.

Kete-Class Interference is variable. Each object holds a baseline stability, but it shifts under use. Stability is driven by Knot handling and object disposition. Stability is tracked on an axis centered at 0. Core Knots move one step towards the positive signal. Broken Knots move one step towards the negative. Without input, stability returns toward 0. Each object has a preferred state. Some favor equilibrium, some, positive values. Others require sustained instability. Alignment yields optimal output. Deviation causes failure. KEEN BAND

Stable regime. Signal is clean, phase-locked, low noise. The object stays aligned and answers clean. Output is precise, capped, repeatable. Push is resisted.

Effects: reduced peak output, tight targeting, minimal distortion, low collateral.

Extreme (+3): Lock. The link goes rigid. Broken Knots fail or are suppressed. Responses dull. Control holds, reach dies. Short line.

BREAK BAND

Unstable regime. Signal is saturated, slipping phase, feedback rising. The object is pushed past limit. Output spikes, warps, leaks. Control decays. Cost builds.

Effects: increased output, distortion, targeting drift, collateral checks (BRN/FRM/NRV).

Extreme (-3): Rupture. Forced backlash. Effects overfire or misroute. The object acts out of band. Draw too hard, it snaps.

0 [ZERO]

Equilibrium. Balanced load. Clean coupling. No bias.

Effects: baseline output, no modifiers. It holds, but it doesn't carry. Just the line, straight and quiet.
