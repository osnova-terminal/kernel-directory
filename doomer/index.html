KIND-II OPERATORDOOMER

ULTRABLACK
ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

§1.1 A Doomer is a KIND-II Operator. Their interface with ESP is Traffick: a direct dive into the OTHER SIDE to bargain and commune with entities dwelling beyond causal law.

§1.2 The OTHER SIDE is herein defined as: the absence of Parameter. The place every Operant model has, to date, failed to return from intact.

§1.3 The dive, the Traffick, and the binding that follows are together herein defined as Banning.

§1.4 Nothing a Doomer does collapses into a measurable form.

§2.2 Black Nail binds, fixes, punctures. It holds a fate in place.

§2.6 Every Kindling is bound to at least one Keep. A Doomer treats with whichever Keep stands behind the Kindling they Bann.

ARTICLE III — KINDLINGS

§3.1 A Kindling is what the Doomer calls out of a Keep and delivers to the field.

§3.2 All eighteen recorded Kindlings sort into three registers: Hard Lock, Red Shift, and Negative Box.

§3.3 A Kindling escalates through three Self tiers — Nibble, Feed, Devour — each stronger, each pricier. The Doomer chooses the tier. The Keep sets what it costs.

§3.4 Every Kindling also carries a Living Demand and a Dead Demand: the same rite, worked instead through a living subject or a corpse, each priced on its own terms.

ARTICLE IV — DEMANDS

§4.1 The OTHER SIDE does not lend. Every Banning is paid, either on the spot or as a Debt the Keep sets aside and collects later, unannounced.

§4.2 A Self Demand lands on the Doomer, always.

§4.3 A Living Demand usually lands on the Doomer too, mirrored, delayed, or split through the subject they worked it on.

§4.4 A Dead Demand is rarely owed at all. In the Operant's records, the dead have already paid theirs.

ARTICLE V — LIMINAL LOCK

§5.1 The Doomer holds no Focal Phase. There is no Parameter form of Kindling to file into an intake record.

§5.2 Phase Reversal is unavailable to this Class. Liminal is the only Phase a Doomer has ever occupied.

§5.3 The Operant maintains no containment model for the Doomer's output. This Article records that absence.

DOOMER FIELD PROCEDURE

On Banning, Kindlings, Tiers, and the Demand the Other Side collects

BANNING PROCEDURE DIRECTIVES

Banning

Banning happens live, in the field, at the point of need, through a Full Action. It is always a Rite Action: uninterrupted incantation, offering, marking, evocation, or utterance, sustained without break until it resolves. This Class carries no Observation Action. The Other Side must be addressed directly and the rite completed, or it goes unheard. An interrupted Rite still owes — the Keep collects a partial Demand for the attempt, resolved or not.


Paying

The Demand is paid at one of two moments: on the spot, the instant the Kindling resolves, or as a Debt the Keep sets aside and collects later, on its own schedule and without notice. The Doomer does not choose which. The Keep does.


Rebanning

A Doomer may Bann any Kindling from any Keep they hold standing with, in any scene, as many times as their body and their debts allow. Marks persist across missions — scars, brands, severances, and unpaid Debts remain until the Keep considers them settled.
